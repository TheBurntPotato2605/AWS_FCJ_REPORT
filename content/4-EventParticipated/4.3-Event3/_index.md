# Summary Report: “AI/ML/GenAI on AWS”

### Keynote speakers

- **Lam Tuan Kiet** - Sr Devops Engineer of FPT Software
- **Danh Hoang Hieu Nghi** - AI Engineer of Renova Cloud
- **Dinh Le Hoang Anh** - Cloud Engineer Trainee of First Cloud AI Journey

### Event Objectives

- **Generative AI with Amazon Bedrock**: Overview of capabilities and use cases, including how Bedrock supports businesses in deploying AI efficiently.
- **Foundation Models**: Claude, Llama, Titan – comparison and selection guide, helping participants understand the strengths and weaknesses of each model in specific scenarios.
- **Prompt Engineering**: Techniques for building effective prompts, Chain-of-Thought reasoning, and Few-shot learning to optimize AI model outputs.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Architecture and Knowledge Base integration to enhance the accuracy and relevance of AI-generated responses.
- **Bedrock Agents**: Multi-step workflows and tool integrations, demonstrating how to automate complex tasks.

### Key highlights

#### Generative AI with Amazon Bedrock
Amazon Bedrock provides a robust platform for deploying Generative AI models without managing complex infrastructure. Participants were introduced to using Bedrock to build innovative AI applications, from content creation to workflow automation.

#### Foundation Models
This section focused on comparing Claude, Llama, and Titan models. Participants learned how to select the right model based on specific requirements, such as accuracy, processing speed, and scalability.

#### Prompt Engineering
Building effective prompts is crucial for optimizing AI performance. Real-world examples were presented, including how to use Chain-of-Thought reasoning to improve logic and Few-shot learning to minimize training data requirements.

#### Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
RAG is an advanced architecture that combines Generative AI with a Knowledge Base. Participants were guided on integrating RAG to improve the accuracy and relevance of responses, especially in applications requiring precise information.

#### Bedrock Agents
Bedrock Agents enable automation of complex workflows through multi-step processes. This section illustrated how to use Agents to integrate various tools and services, enhancing productivity.

#### Other Pretrained AI Services

In addition to Amazon Bedrock, AWS offers a variety of pretrained AI services that cater to specific use cases:

- **Amazon Rekognition**: Image and video analysis for object detection, facial recognition, and activity tracking.
- **Amazon Translate**: Neural machine translation for real-time and batch language translation.
- **Amazon Textract**: Intelligent document processing to extract text and data from scanned documents.
- **Amazon Transcribe**: Automatic speech recognition for converting audio to text.
- **Amazon Polly**: Text-to-speech service for creating lifelike speech in multiple languages.
- **Amazon Comprehend**: Natural language processing for sentiment analysis, entity recognition, and topic modeling.
- **Amazon Kendra**: Intelligent search service for enterprise data.
- **Amazon Lookout**: Anomaly detection in industrial equipment and processes.
- **Amazon Personalize**: Machine learning-powered personalization for recommendations and user experiences.

- **Difference between Foundation Models and Traditional ML Models**: Foundation models are pre-trained on vast datasets, enabling them to generalize across a wide range of tasks, whereas traditional ML models are typically task-specific and require extensive labeled data for training. Foundation models offer scalability, adaptability, and efficiency for modern AI applications.
- **Why Foundation Models**: Foundation models reduce development time and resources by leveraging pre-trained knowledge, making them ideal for applications requiring contextual understanding, such as natural language processing, image recognition, and more.

### Event Experience

The event offered a dynamic and collaborative atmosphere where participants delved into the capabilities of AI/ML/GenAI on AWS. Through interactive sessions and practical demonstrations, attendees gained hands-on experience with Amazon Bedrock and other AI services.

### Lessons Learned

- Foundation models provide a scalable and efficient alternative to traditional ML models, reducing the need for extensive labeled data.
- Prompt engineering and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) are critical techniques for optimizing AI outputs and integrating knowledge bases.

### Key Takeaways

- Gained a comprehensive understanding of AI/ML/GenAI on AWS, including the capabilities of Amazon Bedrock and its integration into workflows.
- Learned the differences between foundation models and traditional ML models, and why foundation models are ideal for modern AI applications.
- Acquired practical knowledge of advanced techniques like Prompt Engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and the use of Bedrock Agents for automating complex workflows.
- Discovered other pretrained AI services on AWS, such as Rekognition, Translate, Textract, and Personalize, and their specific use cases.
