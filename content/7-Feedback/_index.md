### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The working environment at FCJ is friendly, open, and very supportive. Even as an intern, I always felt comfortable asking questions or seeking help. The team members are enthusiastic and willing to assist whenever I face difficulties, which helped me progress faster in my project. The workspace is well-organized and comfortable, allowing me to stay focused. 

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
My mentor provided clear and detailed guidance throughout the internship. What I appreciate most is how the mentor and FCJ members always gave timely feedback — especially when improving my project diagram — and encouraged me to think and solve problems on my own before stepping in to help. The admin team also supported me well with documents and project, making the overall experience smooth and productive.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
The project I worked on, Secure Serverless for Global Applications, was closely related to what I learned at university while also pushing me into new areas. Tasks like configuring AWS WAF, managing CloudFront distributions, and integrating Cognito User Pools helped me apply my theoretical knowledge to real-world cloud solutions and gain more practical experience.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
I improved significantly in cloud-related skills, especially when working with AWS services. I also learned how to work more efficiently in a professional environment — from using project management tools to communicating within a team. The mentor often shared real project insights and organized different workshops, which helped me better understand how to plan my future career path and improve my project.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
The culture at FCJ is positive, respectful, and collaborative. Everyone works seriously but still keeps the atmosphere enjoyable. During tight deadlines or important tasks, the team supports one another regardless of role or seniority. This made me feel included and valued, even though I was just an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**

The internship allowance and flexible working hours were very helpful. I also really appreciated being able to join internal training sessions, which provided extra learning opportunities beyond my project work. In addition, FCJ organizes many workshops and events, which greatly helped widen my knowledge and even supported me in upgrading and improving my project.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  

What I found most satisfying was the supportive and enthusiastic way my mentor and FCJ members guided me. Their clear explanations, quick feedback, and willingness to help—especially when improving my project diagram—made me feel confident and motivated. Working on real AWS services like WAF, CloudFront, and Cognito also gave me a strong sense of progress.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  

The company could improve by offering clearer guidance on time management for the project. Sometimes it was difficult to estimate how long certain AWS tasks or configurations should take, so having suggested timelines or examples from previous interns would help new interns plan their work more effectively.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not? 

Yes, I would definitely recommend it. The environment at FCJ is genuinely supportive, and the mentor team provides clear, patient guidance that makes it easy for interns to learn and grow. What I appreciate most is that interns are trusted with real, meaningful tasks instead of just doing simple or repetitive work. Working directly with AWS services and contributing to an actual project helped me gain practical experience that I wouldn’t get from textbooks alone. Additionally, the company organizes many workshops and internal events that expose interns to a variety of topics, from technical skills to industry insights. These activities really help broaden your knowledge and give you a better understanding of how different teams operate. Overall, it’s a great place for interns who want to develop both their technical abilities and their professional mindset.

### Suggestions & Expectations
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?  

One suggestion would be to provide interns with more opportunities to shadow senior members during their real tasks or decision-making processes. Observing how experienced team members approach architecture, problem-solving, or requirement discussions would give interns deeper insights into industry standards and help them learn even faster.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?  

Yes, I would be interested in continuing if there are opportunities. I enjoyed the environment, learned a lot about AWS and cloud security, and would like to keep improving my skills through more real-world projects at FCJ.
