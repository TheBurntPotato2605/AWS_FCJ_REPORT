### Week 10 Goals:

* Understand and practice cost management, quotas, and snapshots on AWS.
* Learn how to delegate Billing Console permissions, manage service quotas, and automate EBS snapshots.

### Week 10 Achievements:

* Understand how to delegate Billing Console permissions to manage costs and separate responsibilities within the organization.
* Learn how to use **Service Quotas** to check and request service limit increases when needed.
* Practice basic operations in **Cost and Usage Management**:
  * Create cost reports.
  * Analyze usage by service.
  * Use Cost Explorer to visualize data.
* Grasp the process of **Snapshot Automation with Amazon EBS Data Lifecycle Manager**:
  * Create lifecycle policies.
  * Schedule automated backups.
  * Check and manage snapshots.
