### Week 8 Goals:

* Learn and apply **S3 Security Best Practices** to manage data securely.
* Get familiar with **Amazon CloudFront** and the concept of Content Delivery.
* Practice setting up **Content Delivery Setup with CloudFront**.

### Week 8 Achievements:

* Begin to get familiar with S3 security best practices and try applying them to a sample bucket.
* Successfully create and configure a bucket with Bucket Policy, Encryption, and Block Public Access at a basic level.
* Understand the concept of Content Delivery Network (CDN) and the role of Amazon CloudFront.
* Try creating a CloudFront Distribution connected to S3 for content delivery.
* Observe the difference in access speed when using CloudFront compared to direct S3 access.
